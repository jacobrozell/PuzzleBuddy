# Home screen widget

**Target:** 1.2.0, started on `release/1.2.0`  
**Plan:** [1.2.0-extensions-and-tips.md](../../docs/implementation/1.2.0-extensions-and-tips.md)

## In this slice

- **On the table:** the current puzzle (title, pieces, progress bar, timer or days out). When nothing is in progress, it names the next to-do that is not on loan. Tap opens that puzzle.
- **Collection:** shelf counts and a status mix bar. Small adds the top stat line (progress, missing pieces, rating, and so on). Medium shows two of those lines. Large and extra large add this year’s finish chart, a pace chart, and up to four stat lines. Tap opens Stats.
