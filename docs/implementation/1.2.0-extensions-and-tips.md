## Home screen widget

Two widgets share one snapshot.

**On the table** (small and medium) shows the puzzle you should be working on: title, piece count, a progress bar, missing pieces when flagged, and either the running timer or how many days it has been out. A running timer wins; otherwise the latest start date. The footer is the rest of the in-progress count and the to-do count. When nothing is in progress, it names the next to-do that is not on loan and says how many more are waiting.

**Collection** (small, medium, large, and iPad extra large) is the shelf at a glance. Every size draws a status mix bar. Small shows the total, in progress, to do, and the top stat line. Medium shows to do, in progress, done, and wishlist, two stat lines, and a line for finishes this month, pieces, time, on loan, or abandoned. Large adds a twelve-month finish chart, a pace chart (quick, weekend, marathon), and up to four stat lines drawn from the Stats tab: average progress, missing pieces, rating, finish time, usual piece count, pace, replays, busiest month, favorite type, and brand. A tap opens the Stats tab.

The SwiftData store stays in the app container. The app writes a small snapshot into the App Group `group.com.jacobrozell.Puzzle-Buddy` and asks WidgetKit to reload. The widget never sees photos, notes, or borrower names.

A tap on On the table opens that puzzle, including the to-do it suggests (`puzzlebuddy://puzzle/<uuid>`). With nothing to open, it opens the Puzzles tab (`puzzlebuddy://collection`). Collection opens Stats (`puzzlebuddy://stats`). Any other URL is ignored.

**Before a device archive:** the App ID and the widget App ID need that App Group on team `7JT2JB89AV`. Simulator builds can create it with automatic signing. If signing fails, register `group.com.jacobrozell.Puzzle-Buddy` in the developer portal and retry. Do not move the SwiftData file into the group.

Later, still on this release if it stays small: a cover thumbnail on the medium widget. Not in this slice.

## Timer on a To-Do puzzle

Pick next, and any other open of a To-Do puzzle, shows the timer under the summary. **Start timer** moves that puzzle to In progress, sets the start date, and runs the clock. The card says so before the tap. Wishlist, finished, and abandoned puzzles still have no timer.

On the table then features that puzzle, because a running timer outranks the other in-progress puzzles. The widget tap still opens `puzzlebuddy://puzzle/<uuid>`.

## Support the developer

Settings → Help & Legal → **Support the Developer**. Three consumable In-App Purchases. Nothing unlocks. There is no Restore Purchases row.

Payment goes through StoreKit only. An external tip link (Buy Me a Coffee, Ko-fi, PayPal) is a Guideline 3.1.1 rejection. The app has none of those links.

Android’s first Play release is **1.0.0**, and that binary matches the iOS **`1.1.0`** tag. This slice does not change Android. A finished tip is logged locally as `tip_jar_purchase_completed` and is not an Analytics event, so the shared allowlist still matches iOS `1.1.0`.

| Product ID | Name | Simulator price |
|------------|------|-----------------|
| `com.jacobrozell.Puzzle-Buddy.tip.small` | Small Tip | $0.99 |
| `com.jacobrozell.Puzzle-Buddy.tip.medium` | Medium Tip | $2.99 |
| `com.jacobrozell.Puzzle-Buddy.tip.large` | Large Tip | $4.99 |

The Xcode scheme uses `StoreKit/PuzzleBuddy.storekit`, so the sheet loads in the simulator before the products exist in App Store Connect. Create those consumables in Connect and submit them with the 1.2.0 binary. Until then, a Release build with no products shows “Tips aren’t available right now.”
