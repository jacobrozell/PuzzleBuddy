## Home screen widget

Two widgets share one snapshot.

**On the table** (small and medium) shows the puzzle you should be working on: title, piece count, progress, and either the running timer or how many days it has been out. A running timer wins; otherwise the latest start date. The footer is the rest of the in-progress count and the to-do count. When nothing is in progress, it says so and shows the to-do count.

**Collection** (small and medium) is the shelf at a glance. Small shows the total, in progress, and to do. Medium shows to do, in progress, done, and wishlist, and adds on loan or abandoned only when those counts are not zero.

The SwiftData store stays in the app container. The app writes a small snapshot into the App Group `group.com.jacobrozell.Puzzle-Buddy` and asks WidgetKit to reload. The widget never sees photos, notes, or borrower names.

A tap opens that puzzle (`puzzlebuddy://puzzle/<uuid>`). Any other URL is ignored. The app comes forward on the collection when there is no puzzle to open.

**Before a device archive:** the App ID and the widget App ID need that App Group on team `7JT2JB89AV`. Simulator builds can create it with automatic signing. If signing fails, register `group.com.jacobrozell.Puzzle-Buddy` in the developer portal and retry. Do not move the SwiftData file into the group.

## Support the developer

Settings → Help & Legal → **Support the Developer**. Three consumable In-App Purchases. Nothing unlocks. There is no Restore Purchases row.

Payment goes through StoreKit only. An external tip link (Buy Me a Coffee, Ko-fi, PayPal) is a Guideline 3.1.1 rejection. The app has none of those links.

Android’s first Play release is **1.0.0**, and that binary matches the iOS **`1.1.0`** tag. This slice does not change Android. A finished tip is logged locally as `tip_jar_purchase_completed` and is not an Analytics event, so the shared allowlist still matches iOS `1.1.0`.

| Product ID | Name | Simulator price |
|------------|------|-----------------|
| `com.jacobrozell.Puzzle-Buddy.tip.small` | Small Tip | $0.99 |
| `com.jacobrozell.Puzzle-Buddy.tip.medium` | Medium Tip | $2.99 |
| `com.jacobrozell.Puzzle-Buddy.tip.large` | Large Tip | $4.99 |

The Xcode scheme uses `StoreKit/PuzzleBuddy.storekit`, so the sheet loads in the simulator before the products exist in App Store Connect. Create those consumables in Connect and submit them with the 1.2.0 binary. Until then, a Release build with no products shows “Tips aren’t available right now.”
