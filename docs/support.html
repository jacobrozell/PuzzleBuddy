Common topics

  • Version 1.1: Your collection stays on your device. No account is required. Completion history, undo, On loan and overdue, an in-progress timer, and What’s New notes are in 1.1 on iPhone, iPad, and Android.
  • Pick next puzzle: Tap the dice icon on the puzzle list to randomly choose from your To-Do backlog (optional tag and piece-count filters).
  • Backup: Import and export is not in 1.1, and Puzzle Buddy does not upload your collection. The phone’s own encrypted backup can still include it. See the privacy policy.
  • Duplicate check: Tap the barcode icon on the puzzle list to scan a box and see if you already own it — works offline.
  • Scan to add: Add (+) → Scan barcode saves a new puzzle. Suggestions come from your own collection when you have scanned that barcode before.
  • Photos: Puzzle cover images are stored on your device with each entry.
  • Account sync: Sign-in and a Puzzle Buddy account are not in this version.
