Privacy Policy

Puzzle Buddy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

Puzzle Buddy stores your puzzle collection on your device. We do not sell your data. No account is required.

Data we collect

  • Puzzle metadata you enter (title, piece count, ratings, photos, notes) — stored on your device
  • Optional borrower names when you mark a puzzle On loan — stored on your device, not uploaded as contacts
  • Anonymous usage analytics (Firebase Analytics) and crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics). Events are allowlisted and do not include puzzle titles, photos, or names.

Analytics and crash reporting

Puzzle Buddy may send allowlisted events such as app open, collection-size session snapshots, and milestone reaches to Firebase Analytics, plus crash diagnostics to Firebase Crashlytics, to improve stability. You can review Firebase’s policies at firebase.google.com/support/privacy. Puzzle Buddy does not use advertising tracking or sell your data.

Retention and deletion

Your collection stays on this device until you delete it. To remove everything, open Settings → Collection → Delete All Data. That deletes puzzles, photos, completions, and borrower names. Removing demo data only deletes the sample puzzles. Uninstalling the app also removes on-device data.

Barcode scanning

Barcode duplicate-check and quick-add use only data stored on your device. Puzzle Buddy does not send barcode digits to third-party product databases.
