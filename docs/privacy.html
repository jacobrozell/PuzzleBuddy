Privacy Policy

Puzzle Buddy

Last updated: November 2026

Overview

Puzzle Buddy stores your puzzle collection on your device. We do not sell your data. No account is required.

Data we collect

  • Puzzle metadata you enter (title, piece count, ratings, photos, notes) — stored on your device
  • Optional borrower names when you mark a puzzle On loan — stored on your device, not uploaded as contacts
  • Anonymous usage analytics (Firebase Analytics) and crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics). Events are allowlisted and do not include puzzle titles, photos, or names. A timer event may include a duration bucket (such as under 15 minutes) and never the raw seconds.
  • In-progress timer duration — stored on your device with the puzzle

Analytics and crash reporting

Puzzle Buddy may send allowlisted events such as app open, collection-size session snapshots, and milestone reaches to Firebase Analytics, plus crash diagnostics to Firebase Crashlytics, to improve stability. You can review Firebase’s policies at firebase.google.com/support/privacy. Puzzle Buddy does not use advertising tracking or sell your data.

Retention and deletion

Puzzle Buddy does not upload your collection to an account. It stays on this device until you delete it. To remove everything, open Settings → Collection → Delete All Data. That deletes puzzles, photos, completions, and borrower names. Removing demo data only deletes the sample puzzles. Uninstalling the app removes the copy on that device.

The phone can still include that on-device copy in its own encrypted backup: iCloud or a computer backup on iPhone, and Google backup on Android. A backup can include puzzle details, photos, and borrower names. Puzzle Buddy cannot read that backup. Restoring it onto a phone brings the collection back. After you delete the collection or uninstall, the next device backup no longer contains it. A backup made earlier can still restore an older copy.

Barcode scanning

Barcode duplicate-check and quick-add use only data stored on your device. The camera is used to read the barcode. Those frames are not saved and are not uploaded. Puzzle Buddy does not send barcode digits to third-party product databases, and it does not use the advertising ID.
